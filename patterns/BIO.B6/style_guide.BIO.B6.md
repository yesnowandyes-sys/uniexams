# Style Guide: ESAT BIO.B6 Gene Technologies

## 1. Question Architecture
Questions in this domain are exclusively **Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)** with 5-8 options. The patterns observed split into two distinct sub-genres:

*   **Logical Selection (Statement-based):**
    *   **Structure:** A set of 3-4 numbered statements describing a biological process. Students must identify the correct subset.
    *   **Pattern:** Options are combinations like "1 and 3 only", "1, 2 and 4 only".
    *   **Example:** Q1 (Insulin production), Q2 (Dolly the sheep), Q4 (SCID gene therapy).
    *   **Constraint:** The correct answer is rarely "All of them" (except Q6) or a single statement; it usually requires distinguishing 2-3 correct steps from 1 incorrect one.

*   **Deductive Data Interpretation (Scenario-based):**
    *   **Structure:** A text description of an experiment (often involving plasmids, agar plates, and antibiotics) followed by a logic puzzle.
    *   **Pattern:** Often requires deriving a boolean outcome (Grow/No Grow) or a percentage from a set of rules.
    *   **Example:** Q3 (Agar plate growth), Q5 (Plasmid percentage).

## 2. Vocabulary Conventions
*   **Precision is Key:** Questions use specific terms: "Recombinant plasmid", "Restriction enzymes", "Ligases", "Totipotent/Pluripotent/Multipotent", "Differentiate".
*   **Taxonomic Specificity:** Distinctions between "bacterial cells", "white blood cells", "stem cells", and "embryonic cells" are central to the logic.
*   **Action Verbs:** "Insert", "Cut", "Culture", "Differentiate", "Divide by mitosis".

## 3. Calculator-Free Arithmetic
*   **Calculation Type:** Questions involving numbers (Q5) test simple logic and proportional reasoning rather than complex math.
*   **Pattern:** In Q5, the calculation was finding a percentage of a subset: 16 out of 40.
    *   *Observation:* 16/40 is a clean 40%.
    *   *Strategy:* Calculations are designed to be mentally solvable (factors of 10, simple division).
*   **Quantitative Logic:** Some questions may require interpreting base-pair counts (Q6) or percentages (Q5), but these serve the biological logic rather than testing math skills.

## 4. Complexity & Difficulty
*   **Medium Band (B6.1/B6.3):** Recall of process steps (Insulin, Dolly). Discriminator is usually the specific sequence (e.g., "enucleation happens *before* fusion").
*   **High Band (B6.1 - Application):** Plasmid mapping and antibiotic selection logic (Q3, Q5). These require multi-step deduction: "If Gene P inserts into Gene Q, Gene Q is broken. If Gene Q is broken, no resistance. If no resistance, bacteria die on plate."
