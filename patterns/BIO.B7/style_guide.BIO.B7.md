# ESAT Topic BIO.B7 Style Guide

## 1. Question Structure & Formats
*   **Format Dominance:** `Statement Evaluation` (Multi-True-False) is the dominant format (7/8 questions). Students are typically presented with 3 statements and must evaluate combinations (A-H).
*   **Visual Integration:** Questions frequently rely on **Graphs** (4/8 questions). These include:
    *   Cumulative frequency curves (Q5).
    *   Selection response graphs over generations (Q7).
    *   Sigmoid/Logistic growth curves (Q6).
    *   Comparative physiology graphs (Q8).
*   **Tabular Data:** Contextual information is often presented in tables (e.g., Q2 Sickle Cell table) requiring application of logic to specific data points.

## 2. Content Combinations
*   **Cross-Topic Synthesis:** Questions rarely test B7 in isolation. Common overlaps include:
    *   **Genetics (B5/B6):** Inheritance, alleles, dominance (Q2, Q5, Q8).
    *   **Classification/Biodiversity:** Defining species, competition (Q1).
    *   **Data Analysis:** Reading specific values off axes (Q5, Q8).
*   **Context Variety:** Scenarios range from abstract biological concepts (mutations) to specific case studies:
    *   Human evolution/disease (Sickle Cell, Height).
    *   Artificial selection (Maize oil).
    *   Microbiology (Bacterial growth).

## 3. Difficulty & Wording Conventions
*   **Precision Terms:** The questions use specific qualititative descriptors that require exact biological definitions.
    *   "Natural selection" vs "Evolution" vs "Mutation" (Q1, Q2).
    *   "Phenotype" vs "Genotype" (Q8).
    *   "Limiting factors" (Q6).
*   **Subtlety:** The 'incorrect' statements are often subtle twists of the truth. For example, in Q2, saying heterozygotes are the *only* ones passing on genes (false, but plausible if over-focusing on the advantage).
*   **Averages:** Questions often probe the "mean" or "population" level rather than individual organisms (e.g., Q7 checking the mean grain mass vs the oil content range).

## 4. Calculator & Arithmetic
*   **Calculator Status:** **NOT ALLOWED.**
*   **Arithmetic Type:** No complex calculation is required. The math is limited to:
    *   Reading coordinates off a graph.
    *   Understanding shapes of distributions (skew, cumulative frequency).
    *   Proportions (Q3 pie chart, Q7 divergence).
    *   Simple logical counting (Q1 statement combinations).
*   **Strategy:** The barrier is conceptual logic, not number crunching. Students must interpret trends visually.
