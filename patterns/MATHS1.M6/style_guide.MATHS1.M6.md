# Style Guide — MATHS1.M6 (Statistics)

- Module: Mathematics 1 (M1)
- Corpus questions classified under this topic: 14
- corpus_backed: True

---

## Question Structure Patterns

*   **Data Representation:** Contextual information is almost exclusively presented in **textual paragraphs** rather than raw statistical tables (Q1, Q3, Q8). Q9 is an exception, providing a pre-formatted table of summary statistics.
*   **Variable Assignment:** Problems often use abstract variables ($x, y, z$) to represent statistical measures (mean, median, range), requiring students to think algebraically about statistics (Q5, Q14).
*   **Order of Operations:** Multi-step logical hurdles are common. For example, Q3 requires calculating Women who passed (Women total - Women who failed) before calculating Men who passed.

## Difficulty Calibration

*   **Band 1-3 (Foundation):** Direct calculation of mean/median/range from small ungrouped datasets. Basic "sum divided by count" logic.
*   **Band 4-6 (Intermediate):** "Reverse" statistics problems (Q1, Q4, Q6, Q12). Given a change in mean, find the added data point. Also, basic reading of two-way tables (Q3).
*   **Band 7-9 (Advanced):** Optimization problems within constraints (Q5, Q13, Q14). "Largest possible median given Mean=0 and Range=20." Combining distributions (Q9). Theoretical statements about set properties (Q8).

## Wording Conventions

*   **Precision:** "Exactly 28", "Greater than x", "Distinct integers".
*   **Triggers:** Phrases like "What can be deduced..." or "Which of the following must be true?" signal a logic/proof-based question rather than a calculation one.
*   **Group References:** Frequent use of "Group X" and "Group Y" or "Set A" and "Set B" to partition data.

## Calculator-Free Arithmetic Patterns

*   **Integer Solutions:** Sums and means are designed to be integers. Q1 (Running club): $20 \times 28 + 2x = 22 \times 22$. Wait, $20 \times 28 + 2x = 22 \times 22$.
    *   $560 + 2x = 484$. This is incorrect.
    *   Check: Original 20 people mean 28. Sum = 560.
    *   New 22 people mean 30. Sum = 660.
    *   New sum = 660. Difference = 100. Mean of 2 = 50.
    *   All integer arithmetic.
*   **Averages Friendly:** Means are often integers or simple fractions (.5, .25) to allow for quick mental combination (Q9, Q12).
*   **Divisibility:** In Q9, $N_X = 10$ and $N_Y = 20$. The total count is 30, making the final mean calculation $(360 + 960) / 30 = 1320 / 30 = 44$ clean.
