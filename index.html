FAQ

Common questions

What is this site for?

UniExams helps students prepare for the ESAT (Engineering and Science Admissions Test), used by universities including Cambridge and Imperial, by practising with real exam-style questions in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

Are the questions real?

Yes. The question bank is built from real past-paper style questions, not AI-generated filler, and every question includes a full worked solution.

Which topics are covered?

Five modules: Mathematics 1, Mathematics 2, Physics, Chemistry and Biology, broken down into over 100 topics and subtopics so you can practise exactly what you need.

How do I get an account?

Click "Log in" above, then "Create an account" — sign up with an email and password, or continue with Google. Mental Maths and past-paper practice are free; the full generated question bank unlocks with a one-time payment.

Do I need to install anything?

No. UniExams runs entirely in your browser, so you can practise from any device with an internet connection.

Ready to see where you stand?

Create a free account to start with Mental Maths and past-paper practice — unlock the full generated question bank with a one-time payment whenever you're ready.
